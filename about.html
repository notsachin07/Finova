The Institutional Origin Story

Bridging the gap between graduation and financial survival.

Finova was founded at VIT-AP University, Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh, following an alarming realization: our most brilliant engineering, management, and biotechnology graduates were landing premier job offers, only to experience intense financial shock when reading their first salary slip, receiving Form 16, or navigating compounding credit card interest.

Higher education excels at teaching technical theory, but leaves real-world financial adulthood completely unaddressed. We built Finova to plug natively into Canvas and Moodle, guaranteeing every student graduates workplace-ready — with zero additional teaching hours for faculty.
